# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Wisp to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- assuming good intent and asking before concluding;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- owning your mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private;
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind;
- personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions;
- publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, phone number or chat contents,
  without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainers,
using the contact details on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. You can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who
reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and decide on a fair response, which may include:

1. **A private warning** explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
don't follow this Code of Conduct.
